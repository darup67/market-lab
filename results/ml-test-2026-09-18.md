# Chronos + AutoGluon vs. the opening print — 2026-09-18

Pre-registered 2026-09-07 (report.py): fit on the first half chronologically,
predict the second, beat "just use the opening price". Stated prediction:
**Chronos loses to the opening print.** Result: **confirmed.** No model beat the
print on BTC. On the alt series no model beat even the best constant guess.

Run at 1,219 windows per series, below the 1,400 the design called for. That
does not weaken the BTC verdict: the gap is ~36 points, far outside any CI.
It does limit how small an improvement ag-tab+print could have shown.

- Fit: 609 windows (09-05 → 09-11). Test: 548 windows (09-11 → 09-18).
- Every model sees only what is known at the window's open.
- The spot series comes from `target`, the price at each window's open.
  `settled_yes` equals "next target > this target" in 1215/1215 BTC pairs.

Best constant accuracy (always the more common side): BTC 51.6%, ETH 51.1%,
SOL 50.5%, XRP 53.6%. "coin" = P 0.5, a Brier-0.25 reference. Its accuracy
column means "always NO" and should be ignored.

Chronos probabilities are overconfident. On BTC, Chronos-2 calls of P<0.4 came
up YES 61% of the time (n=88). So every Brier score here is worse than a coin's.

The AutoGluon tabular runs had no neural-network model (fastai is not installed).
The ag-ts ensemble includes Chronos-2 and Toto-2 internally.

Re-run: `~/.venvs/market-ml/bin/python ml_test.py` (Python 3.11 venv; system
python3 is 3.7). Raw numbers: `ml-test-2026-09-18.json`.
